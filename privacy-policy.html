Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

NormieVerse (normieverse.xyz) is an open, interactive experience built on top of the Normies on-chain API. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect (very little) and how we use it.

1. Data We Collect

NormieVerse does not collect, store, or sell any personal data. Specifically:

— We do not require you to create an account.
— We do not collect your name, email, or any personal identifiers.
— We do not use tracking cookies or advertising pixels.
— We do not store your wallet address on any server.

2. Blockchain Data

NormieVerse reads publicly available data from the Ethereum blockchain via the Normies API (api.normies.art). This includes token metadata, traits, ownership addresses, and canvas history. This data is entirely public and on-chain — it is not private information.

When you enter a wallet address to look up Normies, that address is sent directly to the Normies API. We do not store or log wallet addresses on our end.

3. AI Lore Generation

When you click "Reveal Lore", the Normie's on-chain trait data (not any personal information) is sent to our serverless function, which forwards it to the Anthropic API to generate a unique biography. No personal data is included in this request. Anthropic's privacy policy applies to their processing: anthropic.com/privacy.

4. Third-Party Services

NormieVerse uses the following third-party services:

— Normies API — blockchain data provider
— Anthropic Claude — AI lore generation
— Netlify — hosting and serverless functions
— Google Fonts — typography

Each of these services has their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

5. Netlify Analytics

Our hosting provider Netlify may collect basic, anonymized analytics such as page views and traffic sources. This data is aggregated and does not identify individual users. See Netlify's privacy policy.

6. Children's Privacy

NormieVerse is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the project evolves. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Reach out via GitHub or on X.

NormieVerse is an independent project built for the Normies Hackathon 2026. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Normies team.
